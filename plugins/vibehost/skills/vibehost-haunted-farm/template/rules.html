Your farm

Every teammate gets a 3×3 plot. Set it up once (name, link, farmer, scarecrow) and your house appears on the village map, with your farm on its own link.

Expand your field

Your 3×3 field can grow a row, then a column — 150, then 400 candy, up to 4×4. Tap "Expand field" under your farm and say yes (✓) — it asks first, since it costs candy. Every crop keeps its place.

For rent

The village

Every farm is a house on the map — something to pinch, a guard on duty, they just stole from you. Tap a house to visit; Village takes you back. Each district has 8 houses; past 8 farmers, switch districts at the top of the map. Moving out puts your lot back up for rent. "Lately in the village", the bar above the map, lists every steal, catch and help.
